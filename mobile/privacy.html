Privacy Policy — GPX Street View app

Last updated: 2026-10-27

GPX Street View is an iOS and Android app that plays back a GPX route in Google Street View. This policy covers the mobile app only; the Chrome extension has its own privacy policy.

Data the developer collects

None. The app has no account or sign-in, no backend server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting. No routes, location or usage data are sent to the developer.

What stays on your phone

  • GPX files: read and processed on your device to draw the route and play it back. They are never uploaded to the developer.
  • Your location: only requested when you tap the location button on the map, and only "while using the app". It is used to draw your position on the map and is never stored or sent to the developer.
  • Settings (time at each point, distance between points, loop, smooth drive, follow): saved on your device.
  • Cookies set by Google Maps are kept in the app's web view on your device, as in a browser. On Google's cookie consent page (shown in the EU) the app answers "Reject all" for you.

Third-party services

To show Street View and the map, the app connects directly from your phone to these services, which receive the data below as they would from any browser visiting them:

These services handle that data under their own policies: Google, OpenStreetMap Foundation, Esri and Cloudflare (unpkg).

Permissions

  • Location (while using the app): to show where you are on the route map, as described above.
  • Internet: to load Street View and the map.

The app asks for no other permissions.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that.
