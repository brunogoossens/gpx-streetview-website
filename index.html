Help

How to download a GPX file

Every major cycling, running, and hiking app can export a GPX file. Pick your app:

How to export a GPX from Strava

For a route you built in Strava's Route Builder:

  1. Open the route at strava.com/routes.
  2. Click the route to open it, then click Export GPX in the right-hand panel.
  3. The .gpx file downloads straight to your computer.

For an activity you already recorded:

  1. Open the activity on strava.com (GPX export isn't available from the mobile app).
  2. Click the … (more options) menu in the top right of the activity page.
  3. Select Export GPX.
How to export a GPX from Komoot
  1. Open the tour on komoot.com or in the Komoot app.
  2. Tap the … (more) menu on the tour page.
  3. Tap Export as GPX.

Completed tours can be exported by anyone; exporting a planned route before you've ridden it may require a Komoot Premium plan.

How to export a GPX from RideWithGPS
  1. Open the route or trip on ridewithgps.com.
  2. Click the Export button on the route page.
  3. Choose GPX as the file type and download it.
How to export a GPX from Garmin Connect
  1. Sign in at connect.garmin.com and open Activities or Courses.
  2. Open the specific activity or course you want to export.
  3. Click the gear/settings icon and choose Export to GPX (or Export Original).
How do I use a GPX file with this extension?
  1. Install the GPX Street View Flythrough extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the extension and upload the .gpx file you downloaded.
  3. Watch your route play back automatically in Google Street View, with a live minimap alongside it.
