Support — GPX Street View app

GPX Street View plays a GPX route back in Google Street View on your iPhone or Android phone, so you can preview a ride, run or hike before you go.

Need help or found a bug? Email gpx.streetview.mobile@codemountain.be. Mention your phone model and, if it's about a specific route, attach the GPX file.

How do I open a route?

Tap Open GPX file and pick a .gpx file. You can also open one straight from another app: in Files, Mail or your route planner (Strava, Komoot, RideWithGPS, Garmin…), choose "Open in…" (iPhone) or "Open with" (Android) and pick GPX Street View. No file at hand? Tap Try the sample route.

What's the difference between Jump and Smooth drive?

Jump shows each point of the route in turn, for the time set in Settings. Smooth drive glides along the road from point to point, like driving it.

Why do some points say "No Street View here"?

Google has no Street View images at that spot (trails, private roads, some rural areas). Those stretches are drawn in grey on the map and skipped quickly during playback.

Playback is slow or a point keeps loading

Each point is a Google Maps page, so a fast connection helps. Try a larger distance between points in Settings, or Jump mode instead of Smooth drive.

The location button doesn't show where I am

Allow location for GPX Street View in your phone's settings and check that location services are on. Your location is only used to draw your position on the map.
